About
World’s developer stack helps builders create products for verified humans and AI-assisted interactions. World ID enables apps to confirm someone is a unique human without revealing their identity. AgentKit helps identify when agents are backed by real humans rather than bots or scripts, letting products grant access, authorization, or execution rights on that basis. Selfie Check offers a low-friction, selfie-based credential that confirms a real, live person is behind the screen, without requiring an Orb. Identity Attestations let apps request verified identity attributes like age or jurisdiction to support eligibility, compliance, and trust decisions beyond simple proof of human.

Selfie and Identity check full access will be available for developers during the hackathon's weekend.

Preferred feedback: Integration experience: how the integration went, time-to-integrate, and any blockers you hit. Ease of integration - where the docs and SDK helped, and where they got in your way. Value of Selfie Check - whether Selfie Check assurance helped you act (block, gate, or step up a user), and how useful it was in practice. Value of the Sybil score - once we enable it in the future, how it factors into your decisions. POH ( orb verified ) vs. Selfie Check users - any differences you observe between cohorts (fraud/abuse, retention, behavior). Overall sentiment - whether you'd want to keep using Selfie Check and expand it across your product.
